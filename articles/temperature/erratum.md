## Notes

The published values had a spurious factor of two in the strain rate
computation (fixed in #26). The strain rate formula also raised only the
y-tilt cosine to the power −2, which made daily strain rates undefined on most
days for units tilting mainly along their x axis, and underestimated them on
other days. The tilt components were also treated as angles instead of sines
of angles. The corrected values fix all three (#69).

Shear strain rates are averaged as the mean, since October 2014, of daily
values for each tilt unit, then averaged over units with equal weights. This
remains a first-order estimate. Units with short records weigh as much as
units recording for three years: UI02 and UI03 only recorded 25 days in
October 2014, near the bed where shear is highest. Without them, the BH1
average would be 1.50 × 10−9 s−1. The average also ignores the vertical
distribution of the units, and the seasonal and interannual variations in
strain rates. Either way, the mean theoretical warming from viscous
dissipation (at most 0.013 °C a−1) remains far below the observed warming (up
to 0.39 °C a−1).
